## Title

## Short description / tagline
A webcam staring contest against owls who cheat. Don't blink. Don't laugh. Don't look away.

## Description
**Can you out-stare an owl?**

Owl Stare is a staring contest you play with your face. Look into your webcam, wait for *3, 2, 1, STARE!*, and hold it until the timer runs out. The owl does everything it can to break you.

**You lose if you:**
- **blink**
- **smile or laugh**
- **look away** (or leave the camera)

**Eight owls, each with their own dirty tricks:**
- **Hoot**, the fluffy beginner: silly faces and a flurry of feathers
- **Professor Plume** reads you terrible jokes
- **Twitchy** brings a fly, more feathers and fake pop-ups
- **Boomer** has a megaphone and no indoor voice
- **Grandma Owlbert** knits sweetly… until she doesn't
- **DJ Night** drops a beat and dances
- **The Twins**: two owls, double the tricks
- **Great Horned Hootington**, the boss: every trick at once, for 45 seconds

Beat an owl to unlock the next one. Your progress is saved in your browser. After each match you get a result card to save or share. It shows the owl and your time, never your face.

**Your camera stays on your device.** The face tracking runs entirely in your browser. No video or image is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can hide your own camera preview at any time with the 👁 button.

## Controls
- Mouse or touch only: tap the buttons.
- Your face does the rest. A 5-second calibration at the start learns your normal face, your blink and your smile.
- 🔊 (top right) turns the sound on or off.

## Phones and browsers
- Needs a camera that faces you and permission to use it. If you said no by accident, allow the camera in your browser settings and press "Try again".
- Tested in Chrome on desktop; made for recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and phone (iPhone: iOS 16.4 or later). Very old browsers without WebAssembly SIMD get a friendly message instead.
- Play in good light, facing the camera. If blinks or smiles are not caught (for example with glasses), press "recalibrate" on the result screen.
- First load downloads about 9 MB, including the face-tracking model.
